# Sources to scan for the Blog Topics job

## 1. The blog itself (style + duplicate-check)

- https://www.bullorbear.in/latest — paginated archive, freshest first
  (`/latest/2`, `/latest/3`, … for older pages). Re-check the first 1-2 pages
  every pass so a topic already covered doesn't get re-pitched.
- Also usable: category pages for AI, Business, Finance, Lifestyle, Money,
  Personal Finance, Politics, Tech, World — and `/trending`, `/hidden-gems`.
- Style reference: `BLOG_TOPICS_STYLE.md` in this folder.

## 2. Money/growth voices (for angle + timeliness, never for facts to lift verbatim)

- **Raj Shamani** — figuringout podcast, X/Instagram. Focus: money
  psychology, wealth mindset, entrepreneurship, India-audience framing.
  Use for: "why does the reader's relationship with money work this way"
  angles.
- **Vaibhav Sisinty** (@VaibhavSisinty on X, GrowthSchool/Outskill) — AI
  tools, growth marketing, startup lessons. Use for: tech-behavior and
  AI-adjacent everyday-friction angles (notification permissions, app
  design tricks, embedded-fintech-in-consumer-apps type topics).
- Both are already on the X account list in `SOURCES.md` — reuse that scan,
  don't duplicate a second pull.

## 3. ramx.in/blog

Personal dev/tutorial blog (Next.js, Go, "automating tasks with AI"), not a
style match for Bull or Bear — thin material, mostly coding tutorials. Only
useful as a weak-signal source for tech/coding-culture angles (e.g. "Should
Students Still Learn Coding?" style topics). Don't over-index on it.

## 4. community.stayingahead.com/c/roadmaps ("Daily AI Updates")

A daily AI-industry news roundup (funding rounds, AI regulation, market
moves tied to AI). Good raw-fact source for reframing a global AI story into
a Bull-or-Bear "why does this affect you" angle — same reframe move as
Stage 2A in `PROCESS.md`. Login-walled beyond the public post list/preview;
use what's visible. Never lift a stat from here without independent
verification — treat as a lead, not a citable source in the final post.

## 5. General trend scan

Reuse the X account list and India trending topics/Google News scan from
the main `SOURCES.md` — same audience, same relevance filter, no separate
list needed.
