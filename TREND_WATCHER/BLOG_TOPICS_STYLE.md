# Bull or Bear blog style — reference for the Blog Topics job

Built from reading the live post archive at bullorbear.in/latest (pages 1-3,
~24 posts, May–Sept 2026). Refresh this file every month or two — pull a
fresh sample from /latest before assuming it's still accurate.

## The pattern

Almost every post title is one of four shapes:

1. **"Why does [everyday friction] happen?"** — a mundane consumer annoyance
   reframed as a psychology/economics lesson. The reader already feels the
   friction; the post explains the hidden mechanism.
   - "Why Supermarkets Never Put Milk Near the Entrance"
   - "Why Popcorn Costs More Than the Movie Ticket"
   - "Why Flight Food Is So Expensive and Yet Tastes So Bad"
   - "Why do Fuel Prices Never Fall as Fast as They Rise"
   - "Why do Indian Brands Often Look Cheaper Than Foreign Brands"

2. **"Why do WE [universal behavior]?"** — turns the lens on the reader's own
   psychology, not a company's. More introspective, less "gotcha."
   - "Why We Wait for Monday to Change Our Lives"
   - "How to Stop Negative Thoughts and Rewire Your Brain for Positivity"
   - (matches the user's own idea list: "Why We Compare Ourselves
     Constantly," "Why We Always Think Happiness Is One Purchase Away")

3. **India-specific money explainer, dated to the current year** — practical,
   numbers-heavy, calculator/tool-adjacent, SEO-shaped ("in India 2026").
   - "Is Gold Still a Good Investment in 2026?"
   - "Best Lifetime Free Credit Cards in India 2026"
   - "The Real Cost of No-Cost EMI, Nobody Tells You This"
   - "India Is Getting Plastic Money"

4. **Breaking India news/scandal, explained** — FACT-dense, numbered
   breakdown, explicit sourcing, often the longest posts (15-30+ min read).
   Politically or financially charged but explained rather than argued.
   - "Is This Scam Bigger Than the 1992 Harshad Mehta Scam?" (Rajesh Exports/SEBI)
   - "Jharkhand JPSC-JSSC Exam Row Explained"
   - "Petrol and Diesel Prices Hiked for the 4th Time in Two Weeks"

A fifth recurring vein cuts across all four: **generational/cultural
commentary**, usually Gen Z-focused — "Why Gen Z Feels Empty Despite Having
Everything," "How Gen Z Is Redefining Investing in India," "Why Are People
So Angry? The Scary Reality of Growing Rage in India."

## Voice notes

- Plain, direct language. No jargon without immediately unpacking it.
- The hook is a question or a flat surprising claim — never a listicle-style
  "10 things about X."
- Every post picks a real, specific, checkable anchor (a report, a price
  move, a court order, a named study) — even the psychology pieces cite
  actual research (e.g. the "pain of paying," fresh-start effect,
  algorithm aversion), not invented stats. This matches the repo-wide
  Never-rule: never invent a statistic, source, or study.
- Categories in use: PERSONAL FINANCE, MONEY, FINANCE, BUSINESS, LIFESTYLE,
  POLITICS. Pick the closest fit per topic; don't force a new category.
- Site tagline (from the homepage): "for readers who want the full picture
  before deciding which way to lean." Every topic should have a genuine
  two-sided or at-least-surprising angle — not a flat "here are 5 tips" piece.

## What NOT to pitch

- Anything that duplicates a post already in the archive — check
  `BLOG_TOPICS_SOURCES.md` §Already covered (refresh from bullorbear.in/latest
  before each pass) and the last ~2 weeks of `logs/blog_topics/`.
- Generic personal-finance advice with no fresh angle ("how to budget").
- Anything requiring a stat, quote, or study that can't be traced to a real,
  citable source.
