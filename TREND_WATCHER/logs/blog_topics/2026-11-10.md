- **Pattern:** Shape 1 — "why does this everyday friction happen" consumer
  explainer.
- **The hook:** Every November, the same flight route quietly becomes

- **Why it's underrated / why now:** India's wedding season formally

- **Real anchor:** Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) price rose from ~₹60.50/litre

  tied to the West Asia conflict) — a real, checkable cost driver behind
  fare spikes. Civil Aviation Minister Ram Mohan Naidu has stated
  on-record, including in the Lok Sabha, that the government will not cap
  airfares for the festive/wedding season or year-round, calling fare

  scheme covering 25 routes (mostly Northeast/South India) as the
