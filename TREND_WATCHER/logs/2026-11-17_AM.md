## Reliability note (read before trusting anything above)

Same wall hit and explicitly logged in each of the last three passes
(`2026-11-14_PM.md`, `2026-11-15_PM.md`, `2026-11-16_PM.md`, and this morning's
predecessor pattern): the WebSearch index does not reliably carry same-day
India news with a confirmable, in-window timestamp. Specific failure modes
this pass:

- `site:` operators were frequently ignored by the search layer — results
  came back from unrelated aggregators (Business Standard topic-tag pages,
  Telegram mirrors of Livemint, NewsX) rather than the named domain.
- Several results looked date-shaped ("November 17") but resolved on
  inspection to **November 17, 2025** (gold-price tables, a Punjab Kesari
  gold/silver piece, a TCS/NITES hearing story — all confirmed dated Nov 18,
  **2025**, not 2026) or to undated page-header timestamps rather than
  article publish dates.
- The most recent confirmable-dated items surfaced across all 24 queries
  were from Nov 9–12, 2026 (a market close report, a YourStory daily roundup,
  a weekly funding roundup covering Oct 31–Nov 6) — all outside the 18h AM
  freshness window, so none qualify as candidates regardless of content.
- No X/Twitter-trending signal was retrievable — consistent with
  SOURCES.md's standing caveat that X is not meaningfully indexed in
  near-real-time via WebSearch.

This reads as a continuing search-index freshness limitation in this cloud
run, not an absence of real news today — flagging it again since it has now
recurred across at least four consecutive passes (11-14 PM, 11-15 PM, 11-16
PM, 11-17 AM).
