## Candidate 1: October CPI print breaches RBI's 6% ceiling, markets slide, rate-hike bets build for December

- First seen: WebSearch, query #15/#19/#20 (multiple outlets dated Nov 13, 2026)
- Story broke (best estimate): October CPI data is released by MoSPI in the
  evening (~5:30pm IST) on the 12th of the following month in past years;
  the market reaction/intraday coverage is timestamped the morning of Nov
  13. Treat the data release itself as having broken the evening of Nov 12,
  with the market consequence unfolding live this morning (Nov 13).
- Raw framing: "India's October CPI inflation print came in above the
  RBI's upper tolerance limit of 6%, prompting a stock market pullback and
  shifting rate-hike expectations to the December MPC meeting."
- Reframed hook: "The inflation number that just broke the RBI's speed
  limit means the EMI relief you got earlier this year may already be
  running in reverse — and economists can't agree whether the hit in
  December is a small top-up or a much bigger one."
- **Score:**
  - 🔥 Trend: 1 — moving markets and the business press live, right now
  - 😮 Surprise: 0 — ICRA had already forecast ~6.1% for October, so the
    breach itself isn't a shock; what's live is the market/rate-path reaction
  - 💰 Impact: 1 — directly about borrowing costs (home/auto loan EMIs) for
    a huge base of Indian borrowers, plus savings/FD return angle
  - ⚔️ Conflict: 1 — genuine, named analyst disagreement: ICRA expects a
    modest 25bps December hike to 5.75%; SBI Research and BofA both flag a
    larger 50bps move; ICICI Bank ties the size to whether crude oil stays
    elevated. This is real expert disagreement, not one obviously-wrong side.
  - 🎥 Visual: 1 — CPI-vs-RBI-band line chart, Sensex/Nifty intraday chart,
    both straightforward for a carousel
  - **Total: 4/5**
- **Still early?** Yes — this morning's market move and the December-size
  debate are unsettled; different outlets are covering different angles
  (market reaction vs. rate-path forecasts) rather than one settled
  narrative. Few outlets so far have combined "CPI breach" + "Dec MPC size
  debate" + market reaction into one framing.
- **Verdict:** PROCEED (4/5)
- Early, but the Stage-3 clock on the underlying data release started
  yesterday evening (~5:30pm IST Nov 12), so that strict 2-4h window has
  likely already passed. The live angle today is the market reaction and
  the rate-hike-size debate, which is why a first-reaction post is still
  justified right now rather than framed as "breaking."
- Fresh angle for Stage 4: the analyst split on 25bps vs. 50bps in December,
  tied to concrete EMI stakes — not just "inflation is up."
