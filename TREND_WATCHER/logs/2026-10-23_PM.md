## Candidate 1: Infosys Q2 FY27 results — dividend raised, guidance cut

- First seen: Business Standard quarterly-results report, dated Oct 23,
  2026 (URL slug "infosys-q2-results-net-profit-rises-6-2-to-7-823-cr-revenue-up-10-2-126102300941_1"), corroborated by same-day BusinessToday preview/results coverage and Multibagg.
- Story broke (best estimate): post-market-close board meeting today, ~16:00–17:00 IST (standard Indian results-announcement timing; this pass ran ~18:00 IST, so the news is roughly 1–2 hours old).
- Raw framing: Infosys's Q2 FY27 (quarter ended Sept 30, 2026) net profit rose 6.2% YoY to ₹7,823 crore — missing the Bloomberg-polled analyst consensus of ~₹7,904 crore — while revenue rose 10.7% YoY to ₹49,244 crore, beating the ~₹49,108 crore consensus. Operating margin held steady at 21.0%; basic EPS rose 8.7% YoY to ₹19.32. The board declared an interim dividend of ₹26/share (record date Nov 3, 2026; payout Nov 11, 2026), up from ₹23/share a year ago. The company simultaneously cut its FY27 constant-currency revenue growth guidance to 1–2%, down from the 1.5–3% range it gave last quarter, while holding operating-margin guidance at 20–22%.
- Reframed hook: India's second-largest IT employer just raised its dividend and beat on revenue — and in the same breath, cut its own growth outlook for the year, the second guide-down this fiscal year. That split verdict lands in the same week peers like Cognizant (reportedly weighing up to 15,000 job cuts, most in India) and Oracle are trimming headcount — so is this just one company's conservative housekeeping, or an early tell on a sector-wide AI-and-demand squeeze that touches far more than Infosys employees' hikes and bonuses?
- **Score:**

  - 😮 Surprise: 1 — a revenue beat paired with a guidance *cut* (not a raise) is a genuine mixed signal, not a routine beat-and-raise.
  - 💰 Impact: 1 — directly affects IT-sector hiring/wage-hike expectations (Infosys alone employs ~300k+), dividend-income investors, and the stock's weight in index funds/retail portfolios.
  - ⚔️ Conflict: 1 — real two-sided read: bulls point to the revenue beat, the raised dividend (₹26 vs ₹23) and deal-win/AI-integration commentary; bears point to the profit miss and a second consecutive guidance cut landing alongside industry-wide IT layoffs — a genuine, not strawman, disagreement on what the number means.
  - 🎥 Visual: 1 — strong candidate for a simple beat/miss table (profit vs. consensus, revenue vs. consensus, old vs. new guidance band) and a dividend-trend line.
