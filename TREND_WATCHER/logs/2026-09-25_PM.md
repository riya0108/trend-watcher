**The debate:**

  grievance and a legitimate near-term consumer-disruption cost are both
  genuinely in tension, which is exactly why the government has apparently
  chosen to work around the disruption (via the payday shift) rather than
  resolve the underlying dispute either way.

**So-what:** if you bank with a public-sector bank, plan any cheque
clearance, loan disbursement, or EMI-adjacent transaction before Sept 27 —
