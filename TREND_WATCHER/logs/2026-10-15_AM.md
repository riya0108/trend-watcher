## Headline finding this pass: the LPG biometric-Aadhaar case finally moved, after 18 consecutive stale checks

This is the first substantive, dated development on this story since it
was first caught on Oct 6 — not a new story, but the first real forward
motion on an item this agent has carried unresolved for 18 straight
passes over 9 days.

- **What's new:** A hearing actually took place on **October 14** (not
  Oct 5 as earlier reporting suggested — the matter appears to have been
  re-listed). A bench of Acting Chief Justice Nitin Wasudeo Sambre and
  Justice Sanjay Sharma issued notices to the Centre, the Petroleum and
  Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB), and public-sector oil companies,
  directing replies within two weeks. **Next hearing: November 18.**
- **New, named, attributed material:** Attorney General R. Venkataramani
  told the court the biometric requirement exists to check "abuse" of the
  LPG subsidy, and assured the bench that persons with disabilities
  "would be taken care of" — without specifying how. He also urged the
  court to wait until the next hearing before considering interim relief.
- Petitioner: advocate Pulkit Luthra, who has a 75% visual disability,
  challenges the Ministry's Sept 17 letter/Sept 19 press release,
  arguing the iris-scan requirement is impossible for the visually
  impaired and others with physical limitations. Anyone who hasn't
  authenticated is restricted to 5kg/10kg cylinders at market price,
  subject to availability.
- Sources: ETV Bharat, Bar & Bench, India Legal, LawChakra, National
  Herald (all dated Oct 14).
