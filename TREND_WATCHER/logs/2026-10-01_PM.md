### 2. The fingerprint scan that decides who keeps their LPG subsidy

Format: carousel (works well for "what's required → who's affected → the
legal challenge → what's still unresolved"); could also work as a Reel
built around the petitioner's story for urgency and empathy.
Story broke: rule effective Oct 1, 2026; Delhi HC PIL ongoing.
Publish-by deadline: within 24-48h (by Oct 2-3) for the enforcement-day
angle; the piece can be refreshed once the Delhi HC issues a substantive
ruling on the PIL.

**Truth layer:**
- FACT: From Oct 1, 2026, Biometric Aadhaar Authentication (BAA) is
  mandatory for previously-registered beneficiaries to receive subsidised
  LPG, per the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas. Authentication can be
  completed at a distributor's shop, via an oil marketing company's app,
  or through a home-visit service. Consumers who do not authenticate still
  receive LPG, but at market price in 5kg/10kg cylinder sizes, after
  registering that choice through an OMC digital channel. The mandate is
  reported to affect approximately 3.16 crore consumers. A PIL has been
  filed in the Delhi High Court by a practising lawyer with 75% permanent
  visual disability, challenging the mandate (including iris
  authentication) as inaccessible and as exceeding the scope permitted by
  the Aadhaar Act, 2016, on the grounds that it is arbitrary,
  disproportionate, and violates Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution.
  The Delhi High Court has sought the Centre's response.
- ATTRIBUTED CLAIM: Per the Ministry's stated rationale (as reported), the
  mandate is intended to prevent fraudulent diversion of subsidised LPG to
  businesses and ineligible households. Per reporting (The Wire), the
  government had previously and repeatedly stated that Aadhaar was not
  compulsory for LPG, before moving to actively enforce biometric
  authentication now.
- INTERPRETATION: The gap between the government's earlier "not
  compulsory" position and today's enforcement of biometric authentication
  as a condition of the subsidy is the crux of both the news value and the
  legal challenge — the PIL is specifically testing whether that shift
  exceeds what the underlying law (the Aadhaar Act) actually permits.
- OPINION: Not pre-decided by this pass — the human writer should choose
  and label the editorial stance deliberately.
- PREDICTION: How the Delhi High Court will ultimately rule, and whether
  the Centre modifies the mandate (e.g. alternate authentication methods
  for people with disabilities) in response, is unresolved and
  speculative — flagged explicitly as a prediction, not settled fact.
- UNKNOWN: The actual real-world failure/exclusion rate of the biometric
  process beyond the petitioner's specific case; whether other PILs or
  state-level challenges are in progress; the Centre's formal response to
  the Delhi HC, not yet filed as of this pass.

  live, unresolved element driving today's news value is specifically the
  enforcement start date plus the pending Delhi HC case, not the existence
  of the rule itself.

**The debate:**
- Side A (fraud prevention / fiscal responsibility): LPG subsidy leakage

  hasn't ruled, so neither side has "won" yet.

**So-what:** this directly affects whether 3.16 crore households keep a
cash subsidy on a staple monthly household expense, and sets a precedent
for how far biometric authentication can be required as a condition for
