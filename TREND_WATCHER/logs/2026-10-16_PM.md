## Full-debate piece — Jio Platforms IPO (completed this pass; price band now confirmed)

**Format:** Carousel (multi-step: valuation-markdown timeline → confirmed
terms → use of proceeds → the two sides → so-what)
**Story broke (price-band confirmation):** October 14, 2026
**Publish-by deadline:** Oct 15-16, 2026 (24-48h from the Oct 14
confirmation) — i.e., **today is the back edge of this window**; flagging
that explicitly rather than treating the deadline as still wide open.

**Truth layer:**
- **FACT:** Jio Platforms filed its RHP with SEBI; price band fixed at
  ₹1,065-1,119 per share; fresh issue of up to ~27 crore shares; issue
  size ~₹30,213 crore at the upper band (reported, per Business Today).
  Anchor bidding Oct 19; public subscription Oct 21-23; listing expected
  ~Oct 28. Minimum lot 12 shares (~₹13,428 at the upper band). ₹2,500
  crore reserved for eligible Reliance shareholders; ₹500 crore for
  employees at a ₹54/share discount. Reliance Industries holds ~66% of
  Jio Platforms; Meta and Google entities hold roughly 10% and 8%
  respectively (as reported).
- **ATTRIBUTED CLAIM:** An earlier, SEBI-approval-stage report (GMP
  tracker site) cited a larger ₹37,700 crore issue size — not reconciled
  with the ₹30,213 crore figure in the official price-band reporting;
  logged as a discrepancy between sources, not resolved as fact. Grey
  market premium (GMP) — per grey-market tracker sites, not an official
  source — reportedly moved from ~₹156 (Oct 6) to ~₹172 (Oct 10) to ~₹215
  (Oct 14).
- **INTERPRETATION:** A GMP around ₹215 against a ₹1,119 upper band implies
  roughly a 15-16% unofficial listing-day premium, per NewsBytes — read as
  market sentiment, not a guaranteed outcome; GMP has a history of moving
  sharply in either direction before listing.
- **OPINION:** (for the human publishing this to state explicitly as
  opinion, not fact) — whether paying ~₹11 lakh crore for a business whose
  IPO proceeds are reportedly earmarked largely for parent-company debt
  repayment represents good value is a judgment call, not a settled fact.
- **PREDICTION:** Reports speculating this will be "India's largest-ever
  IPO" are a comparison against currently known Indian issue sizes, not a
  certainty until the issue actually closes and is confirmed against
  historical records — labeled as a widely-repeated claim, not verified
  by this pass against a definitive historical ranking.
- **UNKNOWN:** Final confirmed issue size (₹30,213 crore vs. ₹37,700 crore
  discrepancy, unresolved); actual anchor investor names and allocations
  (not yet found); whether the reported ~15-16% GMP premium holds through
  to the Oct 28 listing; exact split of IPO proceeds between Reliance Jio
  Infocomm debt repayment and other corporate purposes (reported
  directionally, not as a confirmed percentage, in this or the AM pass).

**The debate:**
- **Side A (bull / for):** A scarce, tightly-held public float (large
  reserved pools for existing RIL shareholders and employees) in India's
  dominant telecom/digital business, with strong anchor-stage grey-market
  demand (rising GMP through October) signaling investor appetite; bulls
  argue the valuation cut from early pitches reflects a more disciplined,
  realistic price rather than a weakness.
- **Side B (bear / against):** A valuation roughly half of what bankers
  were originally floating, with a large share of proceeds reportedly
  going to debt repayment rather than growth, and an unresolved ₹30,213
  crore vs. ₹37,700 crore size discrepancy in the reporting itself —
  bears read this as a story still being shaped by public relations as
  much as by settled fact, and warn GMP-driven enthusiasm has reversed
  before on other large Indian IPOs.
- Why both sides are real: one reads confirmed demand signals (reserved
  shareholder pools, rising GMP) as the market's own vote; the other reads
  the same confirmed facts (debt-repayment use of proceeds, the
  unreconciled size figures) as reasons for caution — neither requires
  disputing the underlying numbers, only how to weigh them.

**So-what:** This is the IPO a huge share of India's retail investor base
(RIL's existing shareholder base) will be eligible to apply to directly,
with a dedicated discounted pool — directly relevant to anyone holding RIL
shares or a Jio/Reliance Retail connection deciding whether to apply.
