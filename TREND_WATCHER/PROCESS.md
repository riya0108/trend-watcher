# Process

The point is publishing while people are still actively searching, not after
a trend has peaked. That means this can't live on a fixed weekly slot decided
a month in advance — it's a standing process, run on demand, layered on top
of (not replacing) the regular content grid.

| Stage | Action | Target speed |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Catch it** | Scan X trending topics, Google News India, and the 2–3 finance/policy accounts in `SOURCES.md` for early signal | Ongoing / each pass |
| **2. Judge it** | Apply the three questions below before committing to a topic | Under 5 min per topic |
| **3. First reaction** | A same-day text post on X or Threads (fastest-to-publish) staking out the *question*, not the answer | Within 2–4 hours of the story breaking |
| **4. Full debate piece** | A carousel or Reel on Instagram once the story has enough shape to explain properly, but before it stops trending | Within 24–48 hours |

## Stage 2 — Judge It

### A. Reframe before you score

Most stories are dry in their raw form. Before scoring, ask whether the
technical/abstract version can be reframed around its concrete stakes for
the audience — same underlying fact, sharper framing. Score the reframed
version, not the flat one.

> "RBI changes a technical banking regulation." → probably 2/5.
> "RBI's new rule could change how millions of Indians use credit cards." →
> potentially 5/5. Same fact. Different frame.

This is exactly the transformation to make before scoring — never by
changing what actually happened (see the Ultimate Rule: if the hook and the
truth disagree, change the hook, never the truth).

### B. Score it — 5 signals

| Signal | Question |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Trend | Is everyone suddenly talking about it? |
| 😮 Surprise | Would someone stop scrolling? |
| 💰 Impact | Does it affect people's money or lives? |
| ⚔️ Conflict | Is there real disagreement, competition, or controversy — not just one side being obviously wrong? |
| 🎥 Visual | Can something interesting be shown in a Reel/carousel? |

Score each 0 or 1. **A story scoring 4/5 or 5/5 becomes a content candidate.**
Anything below 4 gets logged and dropped — don't force 3 topics to fill a
quota; 2 strong ones beat 3 padded ones.

### C. Timing check (separate from the score)

Is this still early — not yet the 5th account/outlet covering it? This
doesn't affect whether a story qualifies (that's the score), only what it
qualifies *for*:

- Still early → eligible for Stage 3 (same-day first reaction) **and** Stage 4.
- Already saturated → Stage 3 window has likely closed; only pursue Stage 4,
  and only if there's a genuinely fresh angle — log it as a slower
  explainer, not an "in the moment" reaction.

## Stage 3 — First reaction

- Platform: X or Threads (whichever is faster to get live).
- Content: state the tension or the open question the story raises. Do not
  argue a side yet — that's what the full piece is for.
- Deadline: 2–4 hours from when the story broke, not from when it was
  caught. If the story broke hours before the scan, that clock is already
  running — say so in the log instead of pretending the deadline resets at
  scan time.

## Stage 4 — Full debate piece

- Format: Instagram carousel or Reel — whichever suits the number of layers
  the story has (carousel for a multi-step argument, Reel for something that
  needs voice/urgency).
- Must separate FACT / ATTRIBUTED CLAIM / INTERPRETATION / OPINION /
  PREDICTION / UNKNOWN explicitly — see `templates/full_debate_piece_template.md`.
- Must present a genuine bull case and bear case, not a strawman side.
- Deadline: 24–48 hours from when the story broke. Past that window, the
  story has likely peaked — still worth writing if there's a genuinely new
  angle, but log it as a retrospective, not a "in the moment" piece.
